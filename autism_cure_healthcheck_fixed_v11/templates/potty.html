🎯 14-day skill-building plan

  1. Days 1–3: Learn the visual routine and practice sitting calmly.
  2. Days 4–7: Follow predictable toilet times and reduce prompts when possible.
  3. Days 8–10: Encourage communication or pointing to the toilet when needed.
  4. Days 11–14: Practice more independence with clothes, wiping, flushing and hand washing.
Progress is individual. Do not force sitting or remove needed supports just to complete the plan.

🌟 Positive reinforcement

Keep rewards immediate, small and meaningful. Praise effort as well as successful toilet use.

Never punish accidents or shame the child. If toileting is painful, constipation is suspected, there is blood, persistent refusal, or another medical concern, contact the child's clinician.
